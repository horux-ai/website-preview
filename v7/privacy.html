Privacy

What happens to the information you send us through this website.

What this site collects

Nothing automatically. This page has no analytics, no advertising tags and no cookies of its own. Nothing you do here is tracked or stored.

What happens when you write to us

The contact form and every button on this site open your own email program addressed to contact@horuxai.com; nothing you type in the form is stored by this website. We receive whatever you choose to write: your name, your email address, your company and your question. We use it to answer you and to carry on the conversation you started.

How long we keep it

For as long as we are talking, and afterwards for as long as our records of client work require. If you ask us to delete your messages, we delete them.

Who else sees it

Nobody outside HoruxAI. We do not sell, rent or share what you send us, and we do not add you to a mailing list you did not ask for.

Client data in projects

This notice covers the website only. Data inside the systems we build for clients is governed by the contract and the data processing agreement for that engagement.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to contact@horuxai.com and we will answer within four hours on a working day.
